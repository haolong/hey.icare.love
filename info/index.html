Work Info

I'm Haolong Hou, a designer in New York studying Data Visualization (MS) at Parsons School of Design.

My work starts from a real problem: getting lost at a subway transfer, news travelling through old newspapers, a virus moving between strangers. I turn the evidence into games, installations and public archives that people can take part in: playing a game, leaving a story, walking around a model.

I work across fieldwork, data pipelines, interactive web, 3D scanning and physical installation, and I build my own tools when I need them. I use AI coding assistants for parts of the implementation and say so on each project.
